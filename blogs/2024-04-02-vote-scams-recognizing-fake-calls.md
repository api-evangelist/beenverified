As election season ramps up, so do the efforts of scammers who aim to capitalize on the heightened political activity. The Pennsylvania attorney general’s office warns of the emerging threat of election scam calls designed to manipulate voters and swindle their money. “Scam artists in Pennsylvania are always trying new ploys to cheat people out of their money,” the attorney general’s office said, often under the guise of political support, drawing attention to the deceit of scammers.
