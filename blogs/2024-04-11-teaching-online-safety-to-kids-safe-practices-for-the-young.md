In the digital age, teaching online safety to kids is as crucial as teaching them to navigate the physical world. The internet presents a myriad of opportunities for learning, socializing and entertainment, yet it also brings a host of potential dangers. That’s why online safety for children is essential because their inexperience and curiosity can make them vulnerable to cyber threats.
