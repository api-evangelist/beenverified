In today’s rapidly advancing digital landscape, cybersecurity remains a concern. The ubiquity of technology in both personal and professional spheres has led to a corresponding increase in cyber threats that continue to evolve in complexity and scope. Breaches that expose sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) can lead to lawsuits, regulatory fines and a lasting impact on a company’s bottom line and credibility.
