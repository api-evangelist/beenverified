Phone harassment is a growing concern in modern society, where our dependency on telecommunications leaves many vulnerable to intrusions of privacy. Those on the receiving end of persistent unwanted calls understand all too well the range of emotions these interactions can invoke–from mild annoyance to levels of intimidation that may escalate to fearing for one’s own safety as well as the safety of family members. Consequences of phone harassment The negative consequences of receiving repeated unwanted calls are multifaceted and can impact victims on both personal and professional levels.
