Understanding a host’s reputation and reliability is crucial to ensuring a safe and enjoyable rental experience. While platforms like Airbnb, Vrbo and Couchsurfing offer convenience and variety, their systems have limitations and only cover certain geographic regions. To bridge this gap, people search sites emerged as valuable tools for renters seeking to vet potential rental hosts.
