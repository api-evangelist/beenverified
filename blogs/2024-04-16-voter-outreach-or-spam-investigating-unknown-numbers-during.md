During election cycles, it’s increasingly common for voters to receive calls and messages from unknown numbers . This rise in communication can often be attributed to campaigns and political organizations trying to reach constituents. However, not all these attempts to connect are welcome or legitimate; some calls are spam or even fraudulent.
