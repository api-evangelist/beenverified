Phishing is a term synonymous with online deceit. Attackers impersonate legitimate entities, luring victims through emails, text messages, phone calls, and spurious websites to extract usernames , passwords, credit card details, and more. One of the most pervasive phishing methods is the use of fraudulent emails that mimic legitimate communications.
