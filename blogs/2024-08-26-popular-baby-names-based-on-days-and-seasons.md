Choosing a name for a new baby can be a cherished decision, often reflecting family traditions, personal interests, and connections to places or concepts that hold special meaning. These days, parents are opting for a wide variety of names, sometimes including special times like days, months, seasons, and even zodiac signs. BeenVerified conducted a study to reveal some fascinating insights into how these special moments have influenced baby naming trends over the past decade.
