In today’s fast-changing digital world, cybersecurity has become more essential than ever before. One of the most pressing threats is Business Email Compromise (BEC), a sophisticated and highly damaging form of cyberattack. BEC has profound implications for businesses, particularly in the tech industry where email remains a pivotal communication tool.
