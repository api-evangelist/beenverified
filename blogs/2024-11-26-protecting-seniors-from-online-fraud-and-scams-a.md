In an era dominated by technology, the Internet has become an integral part of daily life. While it has opened new avenues for communication, learning, and entertainment, it also presents significant risks, especially for the older generation that’s not as familiar with the evolving tech. Online scams targeting senior citizens are rising, with individuals over the age of 60 losing an estimated $3.4 billion in 2023 alone due to online fraud.
