Cybersecurity is now a critical facet of the real estate industry. As people adopt digital tools—from smart building systems to cloud-based property management platforms—their vulnerability to cyber threats intensifies. Advanced technologies can help streamline property operations but simultaneously introduce risks, with any disruption potentially leading to major financial losses and threat to life.
