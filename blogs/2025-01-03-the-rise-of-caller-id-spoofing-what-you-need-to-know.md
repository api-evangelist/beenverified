Caller ID spoofing is a deceptive technique where scammers manipulate the information displayed on a receiver’s caller ID to make it appear as though the call is coming from a different individual or business. By altering the visible number, scammers can trick recipients into believing they are receiving a call from a trusted source, potentially leading to the disclosure of sensitive information or financial loss. In today’s digital age, understanding Caller ID spoofing is crucial due to its significant impact on both personal and business security.
