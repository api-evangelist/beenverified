In today’s digital age, staying connected while on the go has become essential. Public Wi-Fi networks—found in cafés, airports, hotels, and other communal areas—offer unparalleled convenience, allowing us to surf the web, access emails, and catch up on work. However, this convenience comes with significant risks that can compromise your personal information and digital security.
