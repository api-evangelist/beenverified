As we rely more on digital platforms for everything from banking to socializing, securing our online accounts has never been more critical. Passwords are the first defense against unauthorized access, yet surprisingly, many people still use weak, easily guessable ones. This leaves their personal and financial data vulnerable to cybercriminals.
