In a world where nearly everything we do is online, protecting our digital lives has become non-negotiable. With cyber threats becoming smarter and more persistent, it’s no longer enough to just set a strong password and call it a day. Enter Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) —a simple yet powerful tool that adds a crucial extra layer of defense between your personal information and potential hackers.
