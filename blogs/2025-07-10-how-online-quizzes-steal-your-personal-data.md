Online quizzes have become one of the internet’s most addictive pastimes. Whether you’re uncovering your inner spirit animal or taking a pop culture quiz to see how much you know about the ’90s, these simple questions seem like fun ways to explore your personality. These days, they are everywhere: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, and dedicated quiz sites.
